Global data
Data is constructed with python script PSNG_mobility&High_heat-profile_generation_Tool.ipynb from GIS_&_Timeseries_Tool directory, found in GENeSYS_MOD.tools repository. The construction is based on an assumption of almost constant demand for high industrial heat throughout year, with slight seasonal variations taking local time into account; 2026.08.27; Viktor Rosenberg <viktor.jonberg@gmail.com>
