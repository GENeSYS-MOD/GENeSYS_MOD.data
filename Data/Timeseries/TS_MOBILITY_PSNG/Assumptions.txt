Global data
Data is constructed with python script PSNG_mobility&High_heat-profile_generation_Tool.ipynb from GIS_&_Timeseries_Tool directory, found in GENeSYS_MOD.tools repository. The construction is based on an assumption of different diurnal profiles depending on region:
- average: Africa, Europe, FSU and Central & South America
- car heavy: North America and Oceania
- transit heavy: China, Japan and Korea
- hot climate: Asia-Rest, India and Middle East
Average represents an average passenger transport system with greater mix of modes of transport, with increased travel during typical commute time in morning and evening. Car heavy represents system with greater car dependency, assuming even greater peaks. Transit heavy represents system more depending on public transport services with well developed rail systems and high-speed trains, showing a less peaking system. The hot climate profile represents regions greatly affected by high temperatures during day, pushing more travel towards cooler hours in the evening; 2026.08.27; Viktor Rosenberg <viktor.jonberg@gmail.com>
