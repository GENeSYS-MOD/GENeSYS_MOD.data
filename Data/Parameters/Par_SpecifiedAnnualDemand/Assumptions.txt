For EU countries, assumed to keep the same ratio between 2020 and 2050 demand as EU Reference Scenario 2020. Linear growth between.
Calculation is as follows:
Demand_y = Demand_2020 + (Demand_2020*(Demand_REF2020_2050/Demand_REF2020_2020) - Demand_2020)*((y - 2020) / (2050 - 2020))
Mobility (country level, Oct 2026): Demand_2018/2019/2020 = EU Transport in Figures, Statistical Pocketbook 2020/2021/2022,
Tables 2.2.3 (land freight: road + rail + inland waterways + oil pipelines, Gtkm) and 2.3.3 (land passenger: cars + buses/coaches
+ rail + tram/metro, Gpkm); growth ratio = country-specific total transport activity 2050/2020 of the EU Reference Scenario 2020
(ref2020_energy-transport-ghg.xlsx, sheet <CC>_B); UK: EU Reference Scenario 2016 appendix; TR road tkm from KGM statistics.
where y = year for which the demand is specified

Parameters:
	- Heat_Buildings: 
	
	TYNDP 2022 Scenario Report: OUTPUT_ALL -> Scenario: Distributed Energy -> Sector: Residential + Tertiary -> Subsector: Space_heating + Water_heating -> 
	Energy_carrier: All -> Output_type: Energy_demand.
	
	Heat_Buildings = Heat_Buildings_TYNDP22 (all carriers incl. district heat; Heat_District left SpecifiedAnnualDemand in Sep 2025, district heat is set in Par_DistrictHeatDemand). CH, NO, TR, NONEU_Balkan: national statistics, see the row sources.
	
	- Heat_Industrial (Heat_High_Industrial, Heat_MediumHigh_Industrial, Heat_MediumLow_Industrial, Heat_Low_Industrial):

	Definition: TRUE industrial heat = final energy used for heat in industry (process heat incl. furnaces, kilns, electric arc,
	melting, drying, heat treatment, steam; plus space heating and hot water), all carriers incl. district heat (distributed steam),
	ambient heat and solar heat. Electricity used for non-heat purposes (motor drives, compressors, fans and pumps, machinery,
	lighting, ICT, cooling and refrigeration, electrolysis / generic electrochemical processes) and non-energy use (feedstocks) are
	NOT industrial heat; this electricity belongs to Power (see below).

	1) Base year 2018, total true heat per region (PJ):
	   EU countries:  Heat_2018 = sum over industry sectors s of [ (FC_s - EL_s) x hN(c,s) + EL_s x hE(c,s) ]
	       FC_s, EL_s = Eurostat nrg_bal_c 2018, FC_IND_<s>_E, siec TOTAL resp. E7000 (energy use, excl. non-energy use)
	       sectors: IS, NFM, CPC, NMM, PPP, FBT, TE, MAC, TL, WP and "other" = MQ + CON + NSP
	       hN, hE = heat share of the non-electric resp. electric final energy of sector s, from JRC-IDEES-2021 (country c,
	       year 2018), Industry workbook, sheets <sector>_fec: heat = all fuels in thermal processes (excl. process cooling,
	       thermal cooling, diesel motors) + electricity in "Low-enthalpy heat" and in electric thermal processes (electric arc,
	       furnaces / refining and rolling, kilns, melting tanks, annealing, foundries, heat treatment, direct/process heat,
	       microwave and electric drying, electric connection, aluminium/metal processing and re-melting).
	       Non-heat electricity: lighting, air compressors, motor drives, fans and pumps, machinery, mechanical processes,
	       grinding/mixing/forming, paper machine, pulping, printing, cooling, freeze drying, product/metal finishing,
	       aluminium electrolysis, generic electric chemical processes, other-NFM metal production, sinter-plant and alumina-
	       refinery electricity. (Items whose JRC function is mixed are listed in work package 13, computations.txt; their
	       effect is +-3 to 5 % of the total.)
	       https://jeodpp.jrc.ec.europa.eu/ftp/jrc-opendata/JRC-IDEES/JRC-IDEES-2021_v1/
	   UK:            same formula, hN/hE from JRC-IDEES-2015 UK (year 2015; JRC-IDEES-2021 has no UK data), Eurostat 2018 UK.
	   NO, TR, NONEU_Balkan (= AL+BA+ME+MK+RS+XK): same formula, Eurostat 2018 of the country/countries, hN/hE of the
	       JRC-IDEES-2021 EU27 aggregate 2018 (no JRC-IDEES data for these countries).
	   CH:            BFE (2019) Analyse des schweizerischen Energieverbrauchs 2000-2018 nach Verwendungszwecken, industry 2018
	       (Tab. 28): Raumwaerme 14.6 + Warmwasser 2.5 + Prozesswaerme 86.8 = 103.9 PJ. (Check: the JRC-EU27 method applied to
	       BFE fuels 91.4 / electricity 63.2 PJ gives 104.4 PJ.)

	2) Temperature bands (one split for ALL years 2018-2060):
	   share(band) = sum_s w_s x SINTEF_s(band)
	       SINTEF_s(band) = Data/Sources/SINTEF_Potential_for_Steam_Regeneration_in_Norwegian_Industry.xlsx, sheet Calculations,
	       rows 26-34 (from Wolf et al. 2014, German industry): Low <100 C, MediumLow 100-150 C, MediumHigh 150-1000 C, High >1000 C
	         Iron/Steel/Non-ferrous .01703 .05010 .29158 .64128 | Chemical .09744 .08205 .73846 .08205 | Non-metallic .01639 .13115 .37705 .47541
	         Paper .25919 .14120 .59961 0 | Food .41981 .46462 .11557 0 | Machinery .44762 .16190 .24762 .14286 | Wood .79675 .08130 .12195 0
	         Transport equipment .47727 .11364 .25000 .15909 | Textile/Leather .61538 .38462 0 0
	       w_s = Eurostat nrg_bal_c 2018 FC_IND_<s>_E TOTAL: IS+NFM, CPC, NMM, PPP, FBT, MAC, WP + NSP/2, TE, TL + NSP/2
	       (MQ and CON not allocated). The sector "Not elsewhere specified" is split 50/50 into "Wood and wood products" and
	       "Textiles and leather" (it contains products of both, e.g. furniture, musical instruments). CH: weights = BFE (2019)
	       Tab. 31 branch shares of industrial energy use 2018 ("Uebrige" split 50/50 WP/TL).
	   Heat_<band>_2018 = Heat_2018 x share(band). No district heat is subtracted from Heat_Low_Industrial: industrial
	   district heat is part of the demand and is allocated via Par_DistrictHeatDemand / Par_DistrictHeatSplit.

	3) Development 2020-2050 (2055 and 2060 = 2050):
	   Heat_<band>_y = Heat_<band>_2018 x P(y)
	   EU countries and UK: P(y) = TYNDP 2022 Scenario Report (OUTPUT_ALL, Distributed Energy, Industry, Low_enthalpy_heat +
	       Steam_processes + Other_processes, all carriers, Energy_demand) of the same country, value of year y / value 2015
	       (2015 taken as 2018); 2020 = 2015 + (2030-2015)/3, 2025 = 2015 + 2(2030-2015)/3, 2035 = mean(2030, 2040),
	       2045 = mean(2040, 2050).
	   CH: same with the TYNDP 2022 path of Austria; NO: Sweden (TYNDP covers EU27+UK only).
	   TR: EU Reference Scenario 2020, EU27 "Final Energy Consumption - Industry"; NONEU_Balkan: EU Reference Scenario 2020,
	       Croatia "Final Energy Consumption - Industry"; P(y) = value of year y / 2018 value interpolated 2015-2020.
	   Note: the path is the relative development of the TYNDP aggregate, which still contains non-heat electricity; it is
	   applied to the true-heat base as the documented scenario trend.

	Base-year production (Par_RegionalBaseYearProduction, 2018, HHI_* / HMHI_* / HMLI_* / HLI_*): within each band the
	country's technology shares are kept and scaled so that the band sum equals the 2018 demand of that band (Low: demand minus
	industrial district heat = Par_DistrictHeatDemand 2018 x Par_DistrictHeatSplit "Industry" 2018).

	Power: Power (fuel "Power") is the final electricity demand of appliances, lighting and industrial operations (readthedocs
	SpecifiedAnnualDemand). Industrial non-heat electricity (drives, lighting, compressors, electrolysis ...) is part of Power and
	is therefore excluded from Heat_Industrial (no double counting).

	- H2:
	
	Scenarios for EU EnVis-2060 scenarios follow the following rule:
		- Historic data is taken from European Hydrogen Observatory (2023), which is kept towards the future, as this is currently fossil H2 demand which will potentially need to be replaced by renewable hydrogen sources
		- Future data is based on the TYNDP 2022 Scenario Report:
			- EU Trinity assumes the National Trends Scenario
			- NECP Essentials and REPowerEU++ assume the Global Ambition Scenario
			- Go RES assumes the Distributed Energy Scenario
			- The TYNDP scenario results are added on top of the historic existing hydrogen demands
